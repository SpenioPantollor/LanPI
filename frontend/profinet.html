DCP Identify Scan

Sends a PROFINET DCP "Identify All" multicast request on eth0 and lists every device that answers within a few seconds — read-only discovery, no device configuration is changed (DCP Set is not implemented). Works even with eth0 in Passive mode, since DCP is a Layer 2 protocol and doesn't need an IP address of its own to send from.
